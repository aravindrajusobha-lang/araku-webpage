DISCOVER ARAKU

Where mountains, coffee & culture meet.

Araku Valley is a beautiful hill destination in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh. Its green valleys, waterfalls, coffee plantations and vibrant tribal traditions make it a unique destination for nature lovers.
